Trigonometric Ratios in Right-Angled Triangles

SOH–CAH–TOA: how the three side-ratios of a right triangle let us find unknown angles and lengths — from ladders leaning on walls to the angle of elevation to a kite.

Lesson Objectives

By the end of this lesson, learners will be able to:

  1. Identify the hypotenuse, opposite and adjacent sides relative to a given angle in a right-angled triangle.
  2. State and apply the sine, cosine and tangent ratios (SOH–CAH–TOA) to find unknown sides.
  3. Apply trigonometric ratios to find unknown angles using inverse functions.
  4. Solve real-life problems involving angles of elevation and depression.

About this lesson

Teaching philosophy

This lesson is grounded in constructivism, drawing on Bruner's theory of discovery learning. Rather than being told the three ratios, learners drag the angle above and notice for themselves how the ratios change — then confirm their conjecture using GeoGebra and Desmos in the interactive session. Prior knowledge of Pythagoras' theorem and triangle naming conventions is activated before new vocabulary (opposite, adjacent, hypotenuse) is introduced.
